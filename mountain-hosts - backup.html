About Mountain Hosts

A Mountain Host is a proud member of the National Ski Patrol (NSP), part of a dedicated team made entirely of volunteers who give their time and energy to serve the Bogus Basin community.

Mountain Hosts are the friendly, knowledgeable faces you see on the slopes — answering questions, helping visitors find their way, assisting with minor issues, and making sure everyone has a safe and enjoyable experience on the mountain.

Our Mission: To create a safe and fun environment at Bogus Basin Ski Area for every guest — winter and summer.

Complimentary Mountain Tours

Free Guided Mountain Tours

Join one of our experienced Mountain Hosts for a complimentary guided tour of Bogus Basin! Our hosts will show you around the mountain, share tips on terrain and conditions, point out key facilities, and help you make the most of your day. Tours are perfect for first-time visitors and returning guests alike.

Tours are available every Saturday & Sunday at the following times:

🕥 10:30 AM
🕜 1:30 PM

Meet at the main lodge. No reservation required — just look for a Mountain Host in their NSP vest!
